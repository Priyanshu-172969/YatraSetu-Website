## Architectural & Cultural Heritage Composition

The emblem synthesizes iconic Indian architectural traditions, cultural heritage, and geographical diversity inside a harmonious circular emblem:

1. **Dravidian Temple Architecture (Left):**
   - Stepped pyramidal Vimana inspired by the Shore Temple of Mahabalipuram / Brihadisvara style.
   - Finished in warm terracotta and sandstone tones (`#c9652a`).
2. **Buddhist Stupa Architecture (Center-Left):**
   - Iconic Great Sanchi Stupa hemisphere dome with ceremonial umbrella (*chattra*) finial.
   - Rendered in authoritative deep navy indigo (`#022c4f`).
3. **Nagara Temple Architecture (Center-Right):**
   - Towering North Indian temple *shikhara* with sanctum spire and fluttering saffron/terracotta flag (*dhwaja*), reflecting Kashi Vishwanath and Khajuraho traditions.
   - Rendered in terracotta orange (`#d9531e`).
4. **Indo-Islamic Mughal Architecture (Right):**
   - Taj Mahal central marble dome and flanking minarets.
   - Rendered in white marble (`#ffffff`) with soft slate-blue shaded arches.
5. **Landscape & Travel Symbolism:**
   - **Rising Sun & Golden Arc:** Framing celestial dawn over India (`#fda22e`).
   - **Lush Foliage:** Verdant green grove beneath the monuments.
   - **The Setu (Bridge & River Highway):** Flowing navy blue waterway with curved road/bridge marker, symbolizing connection, guidance, and passage.
   - **Traditional Boat:** Handcrafted wooden boat with boatman on gentle water ripples.

---

## Typography & Color Identity

- **"Yatra"**: Bold heritage serif lettering in Deep Navy Blue (`#022c4f` / `#1a2b49` in stylesheets, `#ffffff` on dark navigation surfaces).
- **"Setu"**: Bold serif lettering in Rich Terracotta Orange (`#ec6b0e` / `#d9531e` in stylesheets).
- **Center Divider**: Stylized three-petal terracotta lotus blossom with flanking horizontal divider rules.
- **Brand Tagline**: `Explore • Plan • Experience` in navy blue with terracotta separator dots.
